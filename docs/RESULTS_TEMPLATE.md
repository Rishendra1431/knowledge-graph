**Rule-based conclusion:**

> [[comparison.claim]]

The claim above is generated by a recorded rule: the graph-aware detector is declared better
**only** if the confidence interval for the paired difference excludes zero. Otherwise the result
is reported as *no statistically detectable difference*. This statement is therefore safe to quote
verbatim; do not upgrade it to a stronger claim.

## 9. Claims this study does and does not support

**Supported by the recorded evidence:**

- The prototype detects the labelled attack classes present in the test split with the precision,
  recall and F1 recorded in section 3, on the machine and data described in section 1.
- Each alert can be traced to a dataset record (file, record id, timestamp), the features that moved
  the score, and the observed relationships involving its entities.
- The resource cost of adding graph features is quantified in section 5.

**Not supported:**

- Any statement that the graph-aware model is better than the baseline unless section 4 says so.
- Any claim that an explanation proves malicious intent.
- Any claim of real-world detection capability from a synthetic-dataset run.
- Generalisation to other networks, other attack mixes, or other hardware.
